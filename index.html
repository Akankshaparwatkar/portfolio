THE PERSON
BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I'm Akanksha Parwatkar, a UX designer currently pursuing my MDes in User Experience Design, with a background in Information Technology.

Coming from both design and technology, I've always found myself somewhere in between the two. I like understanding how things work, but I'm equally interested in why people use them, how they experience them, and what makes an interaction feel right.

My process starts with researching people, defining problems, exploring ideas, prototyping, and testing. I enjoy designing interactive experiences across screens and beyond them, especially through interaction design, game design, AR, and tangible interfaces.
